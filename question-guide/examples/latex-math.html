As the number of trees $B$ increases in a random forest: does bias change? Does variance change? Justify your answer.

The variance of a random forest prediction is given by:

$$\text{Var}(\hat{f}) = \rho \sigma^2 + \frac{1 - \rho}{B} \sigma^2$$

where $\rho$ is the pairwise correlation between individual trees and $\sigma^2$ is the variance of a single tree. Explain what happens to each term as $B \to \infty$.
