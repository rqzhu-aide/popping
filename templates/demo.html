How Popping Works

1

Instructor Creates a Course

Each course gets its own space with a unique login page. Instructors set up teams and add students (via manual entry or CSV upload).

2

Students Join & Pick Teams

Students log in with their ID and PIN, then choose a team. Instructors control how many teams are available and how many members per team.

3

Discussion Phase

The instructor posts a question. Students submit written responses and grade their teammates on participation. All responses update in real time.

4

Competition Phase

Teams present one at a time. The instructor selects a team and a question from the bank, starts a live timer, and the presenting team's name is broadcast to all students.

5

Team Grading

While a team presents, other teams grade them on Content, Delivery, Creativity, and Teamwork. All scores are collected in real time.

6

Export & Finish

At the end of class, the instructor downloads a CSV with all student data: team assignments, login times, peer reviews, and team grades — ready for the gradebook.
